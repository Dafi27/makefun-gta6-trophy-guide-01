    Road to the Platinum

    Six stages, in the order to play them. Every one of the 55 trophies sits in exactly one stage, and each stage lists what to do and why it comes where it does. The roadmap is built from the official trophy descriptions alone.

    Missable Trophies — Read This First

    10 of the 55 trophies can be missed: 6 are tied to a single story mission each, 2 need your partner beside you, and 2 are one-shot set pieces. The rest can be earned any time in free roam. Check this page before you start the story.

    About & Sources

    What this guide is

    An offline tracker and guide for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It runs entirely from its own files: no account, no network, no ads. Your checklist lives in this browser's local storage, and you can export it to a file and import it back.

    How the guides were written

    Every guide is written in our own words from the official trophy description and PSN's tracked count for that trophy. Where a specific place, step or trigger has not been verified in the game yet, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one. The game released on 19 November 2026; the guides will be checked against real play as the community confirms them.

    Sources

    • Official trophy names, descriptions and grades: the official list on Exophase.
    • PSN progress targets (such as 42 Shooting Range challenges): PSNProfiles.
    • Guide steps: our own advice from the official descriptions, not yet checked in the game.

    The official list at a glance

    55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. 17 story, 7 collectibles, 31 activities, and no online trophies in the official list. 28 are hidden on PlayStation until earned; this guide's spoiler mode hides story trophies until you earn them.

    Keyboard

    / focuses search. ↑ and ↓ move through the checklist. Space toggles the selected trophy.
