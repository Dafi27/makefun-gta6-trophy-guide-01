Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Stage 1 — Story first. Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission in order. The chapter and named-mission trophies (16) unlock as you go; missions can be replayed from the pause menu.
  2. Stage 2 — Strangers. Clear the 8 stranger strands across Leonida (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and three more). Six trophies, including the silver Strangers & Freaks.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. Races, shooting range golds, drone races, fishing, arcade, bowling and the 50 challenges. Ten trophies; start stunts early, they build up over the whole game.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles. Cards, WAiNK spots, vehicles, areas, meditation spots, garages and the money trophies. Nine trophies; tour the map region by region.
  5. Stage 5 — Combat & chaos. Robberies, tanks, Combat Focus and the Vice City set-pieces. Twelve trophies; bring armour and a fast car.
  6. Stage 6 — Cleanup. Check the tracker for what is left, then finish 100% completion for A Long Road and the platinum pops.

21 trophies are flagged missable: they are tied to a single story mission or beat. Replay the mission from the pause menu if you miss one — nothing is permanently lost.
